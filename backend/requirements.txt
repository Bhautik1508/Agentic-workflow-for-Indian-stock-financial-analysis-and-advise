# lottery: one deploy silently installed pandas 3.0.5 and numpy 2.4.6 where the

# which is the problem -- the next major could just as easily not, with no
# signal until production broke.
#
# The ceilings cap the NEXT major rather than forcing a downgrade, so nothing
# currently deployed changes. Raise one deliberately, after testing.
